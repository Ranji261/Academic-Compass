How Conflict Detection Works (For Viva)

For any two scheduled courses S₁ and S₂ within the same section and weekday:

Collision Condition: Start(S₁) < End(S₂) AND Start(S₂) < End(S₁)

Overlap Window: max(Start(S₁), Start(S₂)) to min(End(S₁), End(S₂))

Concept 1

What is NLP?

Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a subfield of Artificial Intelligence enabling computers to understand, interpret, and process human language text. In this chatbot, NLP maps free-text student questions to pre-curated FAQ intents.

Concept 2

What is TF-IDF?

Term Frequency - Inverse Document Frequency evaluates how important a word is to a document relative to the corpus.

TF(t, d) = count(t, d) / total_words(d)
IDF(t) = ln((1 + N) / (1 + df(t))) + 1
TF-IDF = TF(t, d) × IDF(t)
Concept 3

What is Cosine Similarity?

It measures the angular similarity between two non-zero vectors in multi-dimensional space, regardless of document length.

Cosine Similarity(A, B) = (A · B) / (||A|| × ||B||)

Result ranges from 0.0 (completely orthogonal/unrelated) to 1.0 (identical direction/meaning).

Concept 4

Confidence Threshold & Fallback

Our engine applies a threshold of 0.25 (25%). If the maximum cosine score falls below 0.25, the bot safely returns a polite fallback message recommending relevant topics instead of hallucinating.
